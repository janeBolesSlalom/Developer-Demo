About This Portfolio

This portfolio showcases a collection of public Salesforce Experience Cloud sites that demonstrate the power and flexibility of the Experience Cloud platform. Each site is designed to deliver exceptional digital experiences tailored to specific audiences and use cases.

Salesforce Experience Cloud enables organizations to create branded, connected experiences for customers, partners, and employees. These sites leverage Lightning Web Components (LWC), custom branding, and seamless integration with Salesforce data and processes.

The sites featured in this portfolio represent different industries and use cases, from customer self-service portals to partner collaboration hubs and employee knowledge bases. Each demonstrates best practices in user experience design, responsive layouts, and enterprise-grade functionality.

Built with modern web technologies and following Salesforce Lightning Design System (SLDS) principles, these experiences provide intuitive, accessible, and engaging interfaces that drive user adoption and satisfaction.
